Connect your bank with SimpleFIN

  1. Create an account at bridge.simplefin.org (about $15/year) and link your banks there.
  2. In SimpleFIN, choose New Connection to create a Setup Token, and copy it.
  3. Paste it below. It works only once. Penny trades it for a private access key, which is stored only on this computer.

Import older transactions

SimpleFIN only reaches back about 90 days. For older history, go to your bank's website, find Download or Export on the transactions page, choose CSV, and pick the longest date range. For Bank of America credit cards you can also select PDF statements and year-end summaries, several at once. Connect SimpleFIN first, then import into the matching account. Transactions Penny already has are skipped.
