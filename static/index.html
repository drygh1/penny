Connect your bank

Penny gets your transactions through SimpleFIN Bridge, a small service that costs about $15 a year. You sign in to your bank on SimpleFIN's website, never in Penny. Penny only gets permission to read balances and transactions, so nothing in Penny can move money.

How to connect, step by step (about 10 minutes)
  1. Make a SimpleFIN account. Open SimpleFIN Bridge, sign up, and pay for a year.
    Open SimpleFIN Bridge
  2. Link your banks there. In SimpleFIN, add each bank and credit card you want in Penny and sign in to it. You can add more later; Penny picks them up on the next sync.
  3. Make a setup token. In SimpleFIN, choose New Connection. It gives you a Setup Token, a long line of letters and numbers. Copy all of it.
  4. Paste it here and click Connect. The first download can take a minute. If you were trying the demo, connecting replaces the made-up transactions with yours.

Good to know
  • A setup token works once. If Penny says it was already used, make a new one in SimpleFIN and paste that.
  • Penny keeps a private key, not your bank password. The token is traded for an access key that's stored only on this Mac.
  • Sync when you like. Click Sync in the top bar. SimpleFIN allows about 24 syncs a day, so a few a day is plenty.
  • History: the first sync brings in recent months. For older transactions, use Import older transactions below.
  • Stopping: Disconnect under "Your data" stops syncing and keeps everything already downloaded.

Import older transactions

SimpleFIN only reaches back about 90 days. For older history, go to your bank's website, find Download or Export on the transactions page, choose CSV, and pick the longest date range. For Bank of America credit cards you can also select PDF statements and year-end summaries, several at once. Connect SimpleFIN first, then import into the matching account. Transactions Penny already has are skipped.

Share Penny with a friend

Makes a copy of the Penny app on your Desktop, ready to send by AirDrop, Messages or email. It's only the app: none of your data goes with it. Your data lives elsewhere on this Mac, and Penny checks every file in the copy for anything of yours (a database, your account names or IDs, your bank key) before saving it. If it finds anything, it doesn't make the copy.

Only send the file this makes. Never send the penny project folder or anything from Library → Application Support → Penny; those hold your data.

Use Penny on your iPhone

Your iPhone can open Penny from this Mac through NordVPN Meshnet (or Tailscale), a private network of only your own devices. Penny is never put on the internet or your Wi-Fi network, every other device needs your passcode, and the phone can't change sharing, your bank connection or backups. It's off until you turn it on, and Penny on this Mac works the same either way.

  1. This Mac's Meshnet address. Pick the one NordVPN shows for this Mac on its Meshnet screen.
  2. A passcode (8 or more characters) for signing in on your phone.
  3. Turn it on.

Help

Tap a question to see the answer.

Where is my data, and who can see it?

Only you. Everything is stored on this Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Penny, with automatic backups next to it. Penny sends nothing anywhere except the requests that fetch your bank data from SimpleFIN, and a once-a-day check for new versions (Settings → Updates; it can be turned off).

Is my bank password safe?

You never type it into Penny. You sign in to your bank on SimpleFIN's website, and SimpleFIN gives Penny read-only access: it can see balances and transactions but can't move money.

How do I get my newest transactions?

Click Sync in the top bar. SimpleFIN allows about 24 syncs a day, so a few a day is plenty. The first sync brings in about the last 90 days; for older history, download CSV files from your bank and use Import older transactions.

A transaction is in the wrong category. How do I fix it?

Pick the right category for it on the Review or Transactions page. That changes just that purchase. To change every purchase from that place, or just ones with similar descriptions, click Make a rule under it, or set the merchant's category on the Merchants page. Penny remembers for future transactions too.

What is the Review page for?

It's an inbox for new transactions. Each one waits there until you've checked its category: click ✓ if it's right, or pick a better one. It starts with the last 30 days; older transactions count as already checked.

Why isn't one of my subscriptions showing?

Penny finds a subscription once it has charged 2 or 3 times on a regular schedule for about the same amount. If a company shows up under different names (like "Northwind" and "Northwind Billing"), combine them on the Merchants page with Combine…. Check the Hidden list on Subscriptions too, in case it was hidden.

What does "pending" mean?

Your bank hasn't finished processing that charge. Its amount can still change (restaurants add the tip later). Penny keeps your checkmarks and categories when it does.

Why doesn't paying my credit card count as spending?

The purchases on the card already counted when you made them, so counting the payment too would count them twice. Moving money between your own accounts (Transfers) and into investments isn't spending either.

Is the net worth history exact?

From your first sync on, yes. Before that, Penny works it out backward from your transactions, and for brokerages and crypto wallets from the deposits it sees going into them, so older points are close estimates.

Can I use Penny on my iPhone?

Yes, through NordVPN Meshnet or Tailscale, which connect only your own devices. Set it up under Use Penny on your iPhone. Your Mac needs to be on and awake for the phone to reach it.

How do I get a newer version of Penny?

Open the new Penny .dmg and drag Penny onto Applications, choosing Replace. Your data stays where it is.

Something went wrong. Can I undo it?

Penny backs up your data every day and right before anything big, like deleting data or restoring. Under Your data → Restore a backup, pick a time to go back to; your current data is backed up first, so even that can be undone.
